Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during my initial cosmetic consultation?

Your clinician conducts a comprehensive oral health check, takes a 3D intraoral scan and studio photographs, discusses your aesthetic goals, and provides a clear written treatment plan with finance options.

Do you offer 0% interest finance plans?

Yes—spread the cost of veneers, Invisalign, or implants over 6 to 12 months at 0% APR, or longer low-interest plans subject to status.

Can I see how my smile will look before treatment starts?

Absolutely. Using Digital Smile Design we create a 3D digital preview and, for veneer cases, a wearable trial smile so you can approve every detail.

Ready for your private consultation?

Speak directly with our London or Manchester patient coordinators to arrange your 3D smile assessment.
