Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats does Fovex support?
Fovex natively supports both PDF and EPUB files. Both formats are processed on-device and stored in an AES-256 encrypted vault.
What happens if I delete a book from my iOS Files app?
Your book is safe! When you import a document into Fovex, the app creates a secure, encrypted local copy. Deleting the original file from iCloud or iOS Files will not remove it from Fovex.
What is RSVP speed reading?
RSVP stands for Rapid Serial Visual Presentation. Words are displayed one by one at a fixed fixation point with an Optimal Recognition Point (ORP) highlight, eliminating saccadic eye movement and enabling speeds up to 1000+ words per minute without eye fatigue.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings inside Fovex and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are logged into your Apple ID, your Pro features will activate immediately.
